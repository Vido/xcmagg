Nietzsche has this inconvenient idea: that the *will to power* isn't a craving for a status/position, but the drive to become more fully itself — and that adversity, the unpleasent stuff, is precisely the whetstone. *What does not kill me*, and all that. The part we'd very much like to skip is how power is expressed. He called the one who overcomes himself the **Übermensch** — not a superhero, but a human who stops waiting for permission and starts legislating his own values.

So allow me a cheap and irresistible pun, because thats how I learned in  2010-era golden-age blogs: the developer who survives this particular decade won't be the one who out-LeetCodes the layoffs. It'll be the **überdev** — the *beyond*-developer — who climbs out of this codemonkey station and into the parts we sneer at: marketing, content, business.
